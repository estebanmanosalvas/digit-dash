## How to Play

Type the correct answer to each arithmetic problem as fast as you can. The game ends when you run out of lives — or time, depending on the mode.

- **Correct answer** → advances instantly to the next question
- **Wrong answer** → you lose a life (or time in Blitz)
- **Build a streak** → multiplies your score (up to 5×)

---

## Game Modes

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| ⚔ Normal | 3 lives, per-question timer. Classic mode. |
| ∞ Endless | No lives, no timer. Pure practice with no pressure. |
| ⚡ Blitz | 3 minutes on the clock. Answer as many as possible. Wrong answers cost 10 seconds. |

---

## Difficulty Levels

| Level | Numbers | Time per question |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| Easy | Small numbers, no negatives | 12 seconds |
| Medium | Larger numbers, occasional negatives | 9 seconds |
| Hard | Large numbers, frequent negatives | 7 seconds |

---

## Features

- Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division
- Negative numbers on Medium and Hard
- Streak multiplier up to 5×
- Personal best saved per difficulty and mode
- Sound effects with mute toggle
- Works on desktop and mobile

---

## Run Locally

No setup required. Just download `index.html` and open it in any browser.
